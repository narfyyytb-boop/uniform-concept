Personal data

Privacy policy

This page explains what data is processed on this site and how to exercise your rights, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act.

Data controller

This is a student project, not the official website of Groupe scolaire Sophie Barat. It collects no personal data, so there is no data controller to contact.

Data collected

This is a showcase site: it contains no forms and collects no personal data.

Cookies and local storage

  • No cookies at all: no advertising, analytics or social media cookies.
  • Your “Reduce animations” choice is saved in your browser (local storage); it is never transmitted.

Photos

In the photo of the school, the students in the courtyard have been removed to respect their image rights. The figures modelling the collection are computer-generated design visuals: they do not depict real students.

External resources

Fonts, scripts and 3D models are hosted on the site itself: your browsing is not shared with any third-party service.

Your rights

For any question about your data or this site, please ask the student who shared this link with you.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
