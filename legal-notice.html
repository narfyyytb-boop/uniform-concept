Accessibility

The site has been designed to be accessible to as many people as possible, following the guidelines of the RGAA, the French public-sector accessibility standard based on WCAG:

  • full keyboard navigation, a skip link and a visible focus indicator;
  • a clear heading structure and navigation landmarks;
  • colour contrast checked on all text;
  • alternative text on informative images;
  • a 3D configurator that can be used with the keyboard (arrow keys to rotate, + and − to zoom), with announcements for screen readers; all product information is also available as text in the collection details;
  • a “Reduce animations” option at the bottom of every page, which also follows your system setting.

Compliance status: the site has not yet been audited for RGAA compliance.

If you have difficulty accessing any content, please tell the student who shared this link with you.
